yt-shorts - Privacy Policy / سياسة الخصوصية

yt-shorts is a private automation tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data we access

The app requests only the permission to upload videos (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload) to the owner's own channel. It does not read, collect, or store any personal data of other users.

Data storage and sharing

Authorization tokens are stored only as encrypted secrets in the owner's private automation environment. No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party.

Data deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time from https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
